# Veyra v1.2.1

## 简体中文

- 修复切换到 GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark 等模型后，Codex 主额度卡片可能显示该模型额度的问题。额度记录现在按会话所用的模型归入对应卡片，主额度卡片不再被其他模型的窗口覆盖。
- 额度记录无法对应到任何在线分组时不再改名并入主额度卡片，避免不同订阅等级（例如主额度含 5 小时与每周两个窗口）下出现卡片内容互换。

需要 macOS 14 或更新版本，支持 Apple Silicon 和 Intel Mac。下载 `Veyra-v1.2.1-macos-universal.zip`，解压后退出正在运行的 Veyra，将新版本移入“应用程序”目录并替换旧版本，然后重新启动。

## English

- Fixed the Codex account card showing another model's quota after switching to a model such as GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark. Quota records are now attributed to the card owned by the session's model, so the account card keeps its own windows.
- Quota records that match no online bucket are no longer renamed into the account card, preventing cards from swapping contents on subscription tiers whose account limit has both 5-hour and weekly windows.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Download `Veyra-v1.2.1-macos-universal.zip`, unzip it, quit the running Veyra app, replace the old version in Applications, and relaunch.
