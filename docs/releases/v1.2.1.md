## English

Veyra 1.2.1 fixes quota cards being attributed to the wrong model's windows after a model switch.

### Fixed

- **Quota cards follow the session's model** — after switching to a model such as GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark, the Codex account card could show that model's quota. Quota records are now attributed to the card owned by the session's model, so the account card keeps its own windows.
- **Unmatched quota records stay put** — quota records that match no online bucket are no longer renamed into the account card, preventing cards from swapping contents on subscription tiers whose account limit has both 5-hour and weekly windows.

### Requirements

- macOS 14 or later. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

### Installation

1. Download `Veyra-v1.2.1-macos-universal.zip` from the assets below
2. Unzip, quit the running Veyra app, and replace the old version in Applications
3. Relaunch Veyra

**Full changelog**: https://github.com/yuzio-ai/veyra/compare/v1.2.0...v1.2.1

## 简体中文

Veyra 1.2.1 修复切换模型后额度卡片归属错误、窗口内容互换的问题。

### 修复

- **额度卡片按会话所用模型归属** —— 切换到 GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark 等模型后，Codex 主额度卡片可能显示该模型额度。额度记录现在按会话所用的模型归入对应卡片，主额度卡片不再被其他模型的窗口覆盖。
- **无法匹配的额度记录不再改名** —— 额度记录无法对应到任何在线分组时不再改名并入主额度卡片，避免不同订阅等级（例如主额度含 5 小时与每周两个窗口）下出现卡片内容互换。

### 系统要求

- macOS 14 或更高版本，支持 Apple Silicon 和 Intel Mac。

### 安装

1. 下载下方 Assets 中的 `Veyra-v1.2.1-macos-universal.zip`
2. 解压后退出正在运行的 Veyra，将新版本移入「应用程序」目录并替换旧版本
3. 重新启动 Veyra

**完整变更**：https://github.com/yuzio-ai/veyra/compare/v1.2.0...v1.2.1
